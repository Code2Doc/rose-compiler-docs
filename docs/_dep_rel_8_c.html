Reverses the direction and alignment bounds of a dependency relation.

This function takes an existing dependency relation and computes its logical inverse. The reversal process involves flipping the dependency direction (e.g., changing LE to GE, or GE to LE) and negating the alignment bounds. For equality (EQ) and inequality (NE) directions, the bounds are negated while the direction type is preserved. For directions that represent a range (LE, GE), the relationship is swapped. Directions representing no specific constraint (NONE) or all possible constraints (ALL) are returned unchanged.

Returns

Postcondition
