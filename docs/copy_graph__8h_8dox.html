Collects all IR nodes within a specified subtree.

This function performs a complete traversal of the subtree rooted at the provided node. It utilizes an internal visitor pattern, specifically a subclass of SgSimpleProcessing named GetAllNodesVisitor, to traverse the tree in preorder. During the traversal, every encountered SgNode is inserted into a set, ensuring that the resulting collection contains all unique nodes present in the subtree.
